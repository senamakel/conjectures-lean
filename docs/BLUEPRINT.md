# Blueprint: Schoenfeld's bounds under RH

Target (fixed, `Schoenfeld/Statement.lean`): Schoenfeld 1976, under RH,
`|ψ(x) − x| < √x log² x/(8π)` (`x ≥ 73.2`), `|θ(x) − x| < √x log² x/(8π)` (`x ≥ 599`),
`|π(x) − li(x)| < √x log x/(8π)` (`x ≥ 2657`).

Proved (`Schoenfeld/Main.lean`, steps 1–5): the same three bounds, same thresholds, constants
`1/8`, `1/4`, `9/20`, under Mathlib's `RiemannHypothesis` alone; no `native_decide`.

Proved (`Schoenfeld/PiSharp.lean`, step 6): the target itself, `SchoenfeldStatement`, under RH
and five named inputs (Rosser's zero count, PsiCert, three finite ranges).

Notation: `ξ = xiZeta` (erdos-985), zeros `ρ = 1/2 + iγ` with multiplicity `m`;
`F(T) = Σ_ρ m · min(1/|ρ|, T/|ρ|²)`; `Ψ₁(t) = Σ_{n ≤ t} Λ(n)(t − n)`.

## Step 1. Zero sums under RH (`Schoenfeld/ZeroSums.lean`)

1. `re_eq_half`: every zero of `ξ` has `Re ρ = 1/2`.
2. `zeroSum_hasSum`: `Σ m/|ρ|² = 2 + γ − log 4π` (Hadamard at `s = 0, 1`), `< 1/20`
   (`zeroSum_le`), hence `|ρ|² ≥ 20` for every zero (`twenty_le_normSq`).
3. `kern_hasSum`: for real `a > 1/2`, `Σ m · a/(a² + γ²) = Re ξ'/ξ(a + 1/2)`. The Hadamard
   constant `b = ξ'/ξ(0)` has `Re b = −Σ Re(1/ρ)`, which cancels.
4. `kern_le`: for `a ≥ 2`, `Σ m · a/(a² + γ²) ≤ ½ log a − ½ log 2π + (12/5)/a =: Vb(a)`,
   from `ξ'/ξ = ζ'/ζ + 1/s + 1/(s−1) − ½ log π + ½ ψ(s/2)`, `ζ'/ζ(s) ≤ 0` for real `s > 1`,
   and `ψ(y) ≤ log y` (erdos-985's `rD_bounds`). This Poisson-kernel sum is the zero-density
   input; it replaces an explicit Riemann–von Mangoldt formula (`N(T)`), which is not in
   Mathlib or erdos-985.

## Step 2. The zero sum `F(T)` (`Schoenfeld/Density.lean`)

5. `fmin_tsum_le_triv`: `F(T) ≤ T/20`.
6. `fmin_le_pointwise`: for `T ≥ 2` and every zero,
   `m min(1/|ρ|, T/|ρ|²) ≤ 0.9 ∫₂^{10T} m da/(a² + γ²) + 2 · m T/(T² + γ²) + 2.75 · m 5/(25 + γ²)`
   (cases `|γ| ≥ T`, `6 ≤ |γ| < T` via `arctan`, `|γ| < 6` via `|ρ|² ≥ 20`).
7. `tsum_integral_kint_le`: `Σ_ρ ∫₂ᴬ m/(a² + γ²) da = ∫₂ᴬ (Σ_ρ m a/(a²+γ²)) da/a ≤ ∫₂ᴬ Vb(a)/a da
   = Jb(A)` (dominated convergence, then step 4), `Jb(A) = ¼ log² A − ½ log 2π log A − (12/5)/A − (…)|₂`.
8. `fmin_tsum_le`: `F(T) ≤ 0.9 Jb(10T) + 2 Vb(T) + 2.75 Vb(5)`. Leading term
   `0.9/4 · log² T`, against the true `(1/2π) log² T`: the factor `0.9 · π/2 ≈ 1.41` is the
   price of the cut-offs `M = 10`, `B = 6` in the `arctan` step.

## Step 3. The differenced explicit formula (`Schoenfeld/PsiInt.lean`)

9. `psi1_nat_eq`: for natural `x ≥ 1`,
   `Ψ₁(x) = x²/2 − x Re Σ m x^ρ/(ρ(ρ+1)) + κ x + x E(x) + δ` (erdos-985's
   `explicit_formula_zeta`, Cesàro weight), with `κ + 1 ∈ [−2.6, −1.8]` (`kappa_bounds`) and
   `x E(x) ∈ [x − 3/2, x]` (`Efun_bounds`, erdos-985's `gammaPart_one_bound`).
10. `tW_diff_le`: `|b^{ρ+1} − a^{ρ+1}| ≤ min(|ρ+1| h √b, 2 b^{3/2})` (`integral_cpow`), so the
    zero part of `Ψ₁(b) − Ψ₁(a)`, `b = a + h`, is at most `h √b F(2b/h)` (`zeroPart_diff_le`).
11. `psi_nat_upper` / `psi_nat_lower`, with `h ψ(a) ≤ Ψ₁(b) − Ψ₁(a) ≤ h ψ(b)` (`psi1_diff`):
    `ψ(a) − a ≤ h/2 − 1.8 + 1.5/h + √b F(2b/h)`, `b − ψ(b) ≤ h/2 + 2.6 + 1.5/h + √b F(2b/h)`.
12. `psi_real_bound`: for real `x ≥ h + 2`,
    `|ψ(x) − x| ≤ h/2 + 3.6 + 1.5/h + √(x+h) F(2(x+h)/h)`.

## Step 4. `|ψ(x) − x| < √x log² x / 8`, `x ≥ 73.2` (`Schoenfeld/Psi.lean`)

13. `psi_mid` (`73.2 ≤ x ≤ 2^18`): step 5, `h ≈ 0.447 x^{3/4}`, 15 intervals generated by a
    script (`piece`); `√` by rational bounds, `log a ≥ k log 2 + 1 − 2^k/a`. Worst ratio of
    bound to target: `0.98` (on `[73.2, 95.2]`).
14. `psi_large` (`x ≥ 2^18`): step 8 with `h = ⌈4√x⌉`, reduced to the quadratic
    `1.004 (0.05625 u² + 0.4566 u + 0.887) + 2.009 < u²/8` in `u = log x ≥ 12.47`
    (`F_poly`, `fin_ineq`). Ratio `0.82` at `2^18`, `→ 0.45` as `x → ∞`.

## Step 5. `θ` and `π` (`Schoenfeld/Theta.lean`, `Schoenfeld/Pi.lean`)

15. `psi_sub_theta_le_eighth`: for `x ≥ 599`, `ψ(x) − θ(x) ≤ ψ(x^{1/2}) + 2ψ(x^{1/3})` (Costa
    Pereira, Mathlib's `psi_sub_theta_le_psi_add_psi_add_psi`) `≤ √x log² x/8`, with
    `ψ(y) ≤ log 4 · y + 2√y log y` and `x = a¹²` (ratio `0.91` at `599`).
    `thetaBound_of_RH`: `|θ(x) − x| < √x log² x/4` for `x ≥ 599`.
16. `logIntegral_eq`: `li(x) = li(2) + x/log x − 2/log 2 + ∫₂ˣ dt/log² t`; with Mathlib's
    `primeCounting_eq_theta_div_log_add_integral`,
    `π(x) − li(x) = (θ(x) − x)/log x + ∫₂ˣ (θ(t) − t)/(t log² t) dt + 2/log 2 − li(2)`.
17. `integral_small`: `|∫₂^599| ≤ 33` (`|θ(t) − t| ≤ t`, nine dyadic pieces);
    `integral_large`: `|∫_599^x| ≤ √x/2` (integrand `≤ (1/4)/√t`); `0 ≤ li(2) ≤ 2`.
    `piBound_of_RH`: `< (9/20) √x log x` for `x ≥ 2657` (`0.40` at `2657`).

## Step 6. The constant `1/(8π)` (round 5)

Inputs (tier H, `Schoenfeld/Inputs.lean`, `Schoenfeld/SmallRange.lean`): `RosserZeroCount`
(Rosser 1941, Theorem 19, statement verbatim from IEANTN), `PsiCert` (`|ψ(x) − x| ≤ 0.8√x` on
`[1500, 10¹⁰]`, verbatim from RSBounds), and `PsiSmallRange`, `ThetaSmallRange`,
`PiSmallRange` (the target on `[73.2, 1500]`, `[599, 3·10⁴]`, `[2657, 4·10⁵]`).

18. `ZeroCount.lean`: `analyticOrderAt_zeta_eq_xi` (in `Im s > 0`, `ζ = (Γℝ(s) s (s−1))⁻¹ ξ`
    with an analytic non-vanishing factor), so `positiveZeroCount T = Σ_{0<γ≤T} m` (`cnt`).
    `ρ ↦ 1 − ρ` preserves `ξ`'s orders (`analyticOrderAt_comp_of_deriv_ne_zero`) and, under RH,
    negates `γ`, so `F(T) ≤ 2 G(T)` with `G(T) = Σ_{γ>0} m min(1/γ, T/γ²)`
    (`fmin_tsum_le_two_Gsum`, using `|ρ| ≥ |γ|`).
19. `CountSum.lean`: for `γ > 0` (hence `γ > 4.4`),
    `min(1/γ, T/γ²) = ∫_{(4.4,T]} 1[γ<t] dt/t² + ∫_{(T,∞)} 1[γ<t] 2T dt/t³`; summing
    (`integral_tsum_of_summable_integral_norm`) gives
    `G(T) = ∫_{4.4}^T N(t)/t² + 2T ∫_T^∞ N(t)/t³`. Rosser and `log log t ≤ log t/e` give
    `N(t) ≤ Nc(t) = (t/2π)(log t − log 2π − 1) + 2.463 + 0.3 log t`, and both integrals are
    elementary: `G(T) ≤ Ub(T) ≤ log² T/(4π) + (1 − log 2π) log T/(2π) + 0.65`
    (`Gsum_le_Ub`, `Ub_le_quad`; the constant is `−log 2π/π − P1(4.4) ≈ 0.6385`).
20. `PsiSharp.lean`: step 12 with `h = ⌈2√8 √x⌉` (`T ≈ √x/√8`, `log T ≤ u/2 − 1.0396`) gives
    `|ψ(x) − x| ≤ √x (u²/(8π) − 0.2985 u + 4.62)` for `x ≥ 10¹⁰` (`psi_explicit`; ratio to the
    target `0.90` at `10¹⁰`, tending to `1` from below like `1 − c/u`).
21. `ThetaSharp.lean`: `ψ(y) ≤ 1.0026 y` (`y ≥ 10⁵`) and `ψ(z) ≤ 1.0173 z` (`z ≥ 2154`) from
    PsiCert and step 20, and `x^{1/3} ≤ 0.02155 √x`: `ψ − θ ≤ 1.047 √x`, so
    `|θ(x) − x| ≤ √x (u²/(8π) − 0.2985 u + 5.667)` for `x ≥ 10¹⁰` (`theta_explicit`). On
    `[3·10⁴, 10¹⁰]`, PsiCert and Mathlib's `ψ(y) ≤ log 4 · y + 2√y log y` give
    `|θ(x) − x| ≤ 3.9252 √x` (`theta_mid_abs`, in `a = x^{1/12} ≥ 2.36`).
22. `PiSharp.lean`: the decomposition of step 16, with
    `|∫₂^599| ≤ 33`, `|∫_599^{3·10⁴}| ≤ 14` (`ThetaSmallRange`: `|g| ≤ 1/(8π√t)`),
    `|∫_{3·10⁴}^b| ≤ 3.9252 · (10.15/6.15) · 2√b/log² b` (`∫ dt/(√t log² t)` against
    `d(2√t/log² t)`), and for `x ≥ 10¹⁰`
    `|∫_{10¹⁰}^x| ≤ 2√x r(log x) − 2·10⁵ r(log 10¹⁰)`, `r(ℓ) = 1/(8π) − 0.2985/ℓ + 6.2/ℓ²`
    (`d/dt[2√t r(ℓ)] ≥ (1/(8π) − 0.2985/ℓ + 5.667/ℓ²)/√t` for `ℓ ≥ 22`). The remainder is
    `√x ((2/(8π) − 0.2985) + 5.07/u + 12.4/u²) < 5148`, checked on `[23.01, 24]`, `[24, 25]`,
    `[25, 26]` (with `√x ≤ e^{12}, e^{12.5}, e^{13}`) and `[26, ∞)`.
    `pi_mid` covers `[4·10⁵, 10¹⁰]`: `(3.9252/u + 12.96/u²)√x + 49.9 < √x u/(8π)` for
    `u ≥ 12.82`.

## The finite inputs (round 7)

* `PsiSmallRange`, `ThetaSmallRange` (`Schoenfeld/Check/Step.lean`, `PsiTheta.lean`): the cell
  checker `checkA` over the integers, jumps at prime powers (ψ) or primes (θ), with rational
  enclosures of `log p` (Mathlib's `log(1 − y)` series, 40 terms, `2^{-64}` rounding).
* `PiSmallRange` (`Schoenfeld/Check/Li.lean`, `Pi.lean`): `li(2657)` from two certified meshes
  (`li(2)` from the vendored integrand, then `∫₂^{2657} dt/log t`), then `checkB` over the primes
  to `4·10⁵`.
* `PsiCert` (`SchoenfeldCompute/Psi.lean`, `Schoenfeld/PsiCert/`): `checkA` with `(4/5)√x` on
  `[1500, 10⁶]`; an exact segmented sieve with prime-power exponents on `(10⁶, 10¹⁰]`, blocks
  of about `√a/256`, guards `ψ(b) − a ≤ (4/5)√a`, `b − ψ(a) ≤ (4/5)√a`. 20 chunks; about
  14 CPU-minutes compiled.

## What remains for an unconditional `1/(8π)`

* `RosserZeroCount`: proved in Lean (`TernaryGoldbach.RSBounds.Rosser1941.zero_count_bounds`,
  teorth/IEANTN `Solutions/Schoenfeld1976Numerics.v1`, Lean `v4.34.0-rc2`, Mathlib `71a80585`):
  219 files, about 95,000 lines in its import closure: 113 files / 24,844 lines for `T ≥ 100`
  (Backlund, Jensen, digamma and Stirling estimates; Mathlib only), 106 files / about 70,000
  lines for the 29 Hardy-function zero certificates below height 100. No `sorry`, `axiom` or
  `native_decide`; licences Apache-2.0 and MIT. Port from the Lean `v4.35.0` originals
  (Garald1/RSBounds `82d7a55`, calr3/ternary-goldbach `192ca57`): of 14 sampled files, 8
  compiled unchanged and 6 needed 10 small fixes (a changed Jensen API, `whnf` timeouts from
  lemmas restated for `f / g`). Estimate: 2–3 days for `T ≥ 100`, 3–6 more for the
  certificates.
* Only the upper half of Rosser's bound is used, through the constant of `Gsum_le_Ub`. By a
  floating-point model of the budget, any `N(T) ≤ main + 7/8 + a log T + b` for all `T ≥ 4.4`
  with `2.48 a + b ≤ 3.31` suffices (Rosser: `2.33`; the binding case is π just above `10¹⁰`).
  A bound valid only for `T ≥ 100` also needs a count below 100 (for instance `N(50) ≤ 10`).
